About Halvane

We test imaging hardware where it is actually used - and we publish the files so you can check our work.

What we believe

A camera review that cannot be checked is an opinion. Since 2003 Halvane has shipped every evaluation with the files behind it, untouched, so that clients can verify capture times, devices and settings for themselves.

That habit grew into a second discipline: reading what files say about themselves. Today our Provenance & Metadata Assurance practice audits imagery for newsrooms, insurers and legal teams.

Founders

Maren Halvik and Teodor Vane met as camera service engineers. The company name is half of each surname.

Timeline

  1. 2003

    Maren Halvik and Teodor Vane start testing cameras from a kitchen table in Copenhagen. Teodor's western Norway road trip produces the first Archive Zero frames.

  2. 2006

    Halvane Imaging Labs ApS is incorporated. First paying clients: two picture agencies and a museum.

  3. 2010

    Felix Brandauer joins; the Alpine & Nordic Light Programme begins with a glacial-lake colour study.

  4. 2012

    Grace Adeyemi introduces the Field Kit Library: every checkout logged against a name and date.

  5. 2014

    Kenji Moriwaki builds the colour bench. Felix crosses the Inside Passage with a wide zoom.

  6. 2015

    The Florence Mobile Imaging Lab opens under Lucia Ferrante; the Smartphone Field Panel begins.

  7. 2016

    Dana Whitlock opens the Boulder office for aerial and action systems.

  8. 2019

    Move to the Nordhavn studio. Ingest 3.0 stamps the workstation into every file's edit history.

  9. 2021

    Provenance & Metadata Assurance becomes a service, led by Amara Osei.

  10. 2025

    TIDEMARK-25 field week for Fellstrand Mutual (May). Internal review of the report imagery opens in October.

  11. 2026

    The Field Kit Library register is made public.
