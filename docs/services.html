What we do

Services

Five practices, one rule: the files ship with the findings.

Field evaluation

We take cameras, lenses and phones where your people will use them: harbours, glaciers, building sites and night streets. Every evaluation ships with the untouched files so you can check our working.

Colour bench & calibration

Reflective and transmissive targets, controlled still lifes and repeatable lighting. We profile bodies for archives, museums and e-commerce studios.

Mobile imaging lab

From Florence, we benchmark smartphones and tablets as people actually use them: auto mode, default app, the scene in front of them.

Aerial & action systems

Drone and action-camera evaluations for surveyors, insurers and broadcasters, including vibration and stabilisation testing.

Provenance & metadata assurance

Independent audits of image metadata for newsrooms, insurers and legal teams: capture-time verification, device attribution, location checks and edit-history review.

How an engagement works

  1. Scoping call and kit list (drawn from the Field Kit Library).
  2. Field and bench capture, logged per device and per day.
  3. Ingest on a named workstation; edit history preserved in every file.
  4. Report with figures, plus the original files and their SHA-256 checksums.
