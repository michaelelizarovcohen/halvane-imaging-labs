TIDEMARK-25

Field-kit evaluation for claims documentation in inland flood and storm scenarios. Report imagery under review.

TIDEMARK-25 evaluated drones and rugged cameras for documenting inland flood and storm claims for Fellstrand Mutual. The field week ran from Monday 5 to Friday 9 May 2025 across Thuringia and northern Hesse, led by Jonas Ekdahl.

Report v1 (two figures) was delivered on 30 May 2025. Report v2, adding figures 3 to 7 to cover days 1, 3, 4 and 5, was delivered on 20 June 2025.

In October 2025 Fellstrand Mutual raised questions about the v2 imagery. The figures below are published unchanged, exactly as delivered, so that the review can be checked independently. See the statement of 28 October 2025.
