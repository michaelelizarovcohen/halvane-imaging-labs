Aerial & Action Systems

Drones, action cameras and the vibration rig that keeps them honest.

Boulder evaluates the cameras that move: quadcopters, foldable drones, action cameras and phones shooting through aircraft windows. Positioning data matters as much as pixels here, so every file is checked for GPS, altitude and time consistency before it enters a report.

The autumn 2021 rugged-kit tour (with Felix) ran from a lake beside a motorway in Occitanie to industrial heritage in the Ruhr, a week apart.
