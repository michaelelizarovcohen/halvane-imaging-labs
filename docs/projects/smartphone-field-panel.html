Smartphone Field Panel

Dozens of handsets, the same kinds of scenes, auto mode only. The Florence lab's founding study.

The Smartphone Field Panel asked a simple question: what does a typical phone actually record when a typical person points it at a typical scene? We collected handsets across brands and price points and photographed campus buildings, markets, corridors, fields and vineyards around Florence and in the Mugello hills, always in auto mode, always in the default camera app.

Several panel days used multiple handsets at once. On 22 August 2015 Lucia and Matteo walked a stretch of the Mugello railway with four devices - a day still used to teach multi-device timeline reconstruction.

Panel files are published exactly as recorded, with descriptive and rights metadata added during ingest.
