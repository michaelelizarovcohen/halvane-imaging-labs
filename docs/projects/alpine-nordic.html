Alpine & Nordic Light Programme

Long-term study of low sun, snow, haze and water across mountain and northern environments.

Bright snow under low sun, haze over long lakes and the blue hour that lasts all night in a northern summer are hard on sensors and harder on phone processing. Since 2010 the Alpine & Nordic Light Programme has collected reference scenes in exactly those conditions.

The programme mixes dedicated cameras with phones running stock and third-party camera apps, so we can compare what the hardware records with what the software decides to show you.
