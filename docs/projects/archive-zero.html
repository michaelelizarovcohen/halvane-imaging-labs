Archive Zero

The founders' reference set: the frames that defined Halvane's first test procedures.

Before Halvane had clients it had an archive. Archive Zero collects the frames Maren and Teodor used to build our first evaluation procedures: a fjord shot on a compact in 2003, a high valley on a first professional body, a harbour comparison between two very different sensors, and Pixel, the studio dog, who features in rather too many white-balance tests.

The set is deliberately heterogeneous: compacts, rangefinders, DSLRs, a film scanner and a cinema camera. Some files carry rich maker notes; one carries almost nothing at all. That variety is the point - our procedures had to work on all of them.

The archive is read-only. New material is never added to Archive Zero; derived copies get new asset numbers.
