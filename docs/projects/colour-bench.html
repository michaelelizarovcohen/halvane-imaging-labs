Colour Bench & Still-Life Series

Charts, targets and still lifes: the repeatable half of every evaluation.

Every body that enters the bench meets the same targets: a reflective IT8, a portable colour checker, a resolution chart and a rotating cast of still lifes chosen to break demosaicing algorithms - saturated reds, metallic highlights, fine repetitive texture.

Bench files are ingested on the bench workstation and keep their full maker notes. Owner fields are set in-camera where the body supports it.
