New studio, new ingest desk

By Grace Adeyemi

We have moved into our new Nordhavn studio. The move also brings Ingest 3.0: every file now passes through a named ingest workstation, and the workstation name is stamped into the file's edit history.

Workstations are numbered by desk. CPH-WS-02 sits with the field engineers, CPH-WS-05 is the colour bench, and CPH-WS-17, by the window, is the general ingest desk used for archive work and by our interns.
