14 September 2015

The Florence Mobile Imaging Lab is open

By Lucia Ferrante

Today we open our second lab, in Florence, dedicated to smartphones and tablets. Our first study, the Smartphone Field Panel, is already under way: one scene, many handsets, auto mode only.

Summer field days in the Mugello hills gave us our first reference sets, including a four-handset rail walk on 22 August.
