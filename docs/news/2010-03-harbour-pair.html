15 March 2010

Archive note: the harbour pair

By Teodor Vane

Our spring newsletter features a comparison we are rather proud of: the same winter quay, photographed the same afternoon with a layered-sensor compact and a conventional Bayer DSLR.

The compact resolves the rigging of the tall ship with almost unsettling crispness; the DSLR wins on colour in the shadows. Neither wins on battery life. Both frames are now part of Archive Zero.
