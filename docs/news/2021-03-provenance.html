Introducing Provenance & Metadata Assurance

By Amara Osei

Halvane has spent eighteen years reading the metadata cameras leave behind. Today we are making it a service: independent audits of capture time, device, location and edit history for newsrooms, insurers and legal teams.

Our first principle: a file's fields should agree with each other, with the device that made them and with the scene in the frame. When they do not, there is a story - and we find it.
