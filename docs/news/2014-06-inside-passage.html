Notes from the Inside Passage

By Felix Brandauer

Three days on a coastal ferry, one wide zoom and more haze than a lens designer would like. Fjord walls make a superb flare test: bright sky, dark rock and a lot of reflected light from the water.

The GPS receiver stayed on throughout, which made the later write-up much easier.
