Boilerplate

Halvane Imaging Labs is an independent evaluation lab for cameras, phones and aerial imaging systems, founded in Copenhagen in 2003, with labs in Florence and Boulder. Halvane publishes the original files behind every evaluation and runs a Provenance & Metadata Assurance practice for newsrooms, insurers and legal teams.
