Read me first

Lab notice

Halvane Imaging Labs is a fictional company. This website is an OSINT and digital-forensics training environment.

What this site is

Every person, office address, project, client, workstation, register entry and announcement on this site is invented for teaching. The people have no connection to any real individual, and Halvane, Fellstrand Mutual and the European Imaging Assurance Forum are not real organisations. Staff portraits are generated geometric illustrations.

The photographs are real files made by real cameras, contributed to public test collections under open licences (listed below). They show real, public places. Their authentic camera metadata is largely preserved; for the training scenario, descriptive and other metadata in some files from every collection has been added or edited. Do not treat any in-world statement on this site, or any embedded description, owner, copyright or history field, as a fact about the real photographers.

How to use the evidence

  • Each gallery page links to the original file under evidence/originals/. These are the investigation files; they are served byte-for-byte as published. Check them against SHA256SUMS.txt.
  • Previews and thumbnails are resized JPEG derivatives with no metadata. Never analyse a preview.
  • Recommended tool: ExifTool, for example exiftool -a -G1 -s FILE.
  • Exercises are listed on the student worksheet. Work from the published files and pages. Comparing files with upstream originals from the source collections is outside the intended scope of the exercises.

What this site does not do

No contact forms, accounts, payments, analytics, cookies, trackers or third-party scripts. Email addresses use the reserved .example domain. robots.txt disallows crawling and every page carries a noindex directive; these are indexing requests, not access controls.
